## Key grounded takeaways

### 1. The edge moved from intelligence to deployment
The video’s central thesis is that frontier-model access is increasingly commoditized. The durable edge is understanding where, how, and why AI should be deployed inside real business workflows.

### 2. FDE is a hybrid role
A high-end FDE combines:
- strong communication and workflow diagnosis
- strong technical execution across models, APIs, evals, and systems

### 3. The real operating loop is:
- Audit
- Eval
- Deployment

This loop repeats continuously as each solved workflow reveals the next bottleneck.

### 4. Workflow reality matters more than SOPs
The talk stresses that the documented workflow is rarely the real workflow. Tribal knowledge, exceptions, and shadow steps are where a lot of implementation risk lives.

### 5. Build on top of existing systems
The recommended deployment posture is integration-first, not rip-and-replace. Work with the customer’s real stack and guide them through shadow mode and gradual autonomy.

### 6. Evidence beats title
The best path into FDE work is to do the work before you have the title: build one real system, harden it, measure it, and package the business case.
